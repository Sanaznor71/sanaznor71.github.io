BACKGROUND

About me

I am a Ph.D. researcher in autonomous driving and control with experience spanning data-driven methods, classical control theory, and experimental systems.

Research approach

I am interested in autonomy that works beyond simulation—models, controllers, and planning systems that can be tested, understood, and improved using measured vehicle behavior.

My current work combines learning-based path planning and model predictive control, with a focus on practical evaluation. Before focusing on autonomous vehicles, I worked on nonlinear control and hardware-in-the-loop validation of spacecraft systems.

Outside individual projects, I enjoy research collaborations and mentoring engineering students.
