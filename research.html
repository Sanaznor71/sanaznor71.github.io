SELECTED RESEARCH

Research & projects

My research combines learning-based methods, optimization, and control with simulation and experimental validation. The selection below reflects my academic CV and publicly documented work.

LEARNING-BASED AUTONOMOUS DRIVING · 2023–PRESENT

End-to-end curvature prediction at intersections

A multi-modal neural network trained on GPS-derived curvature labels from real-world driving data. I worked on data preparation, training, error analysis, ONNX export, and on-vehicle evaluation using Comma 3X and a Nissan Leaf.

Models, datasets, and testing were evaluated across diverse campus routes, including challenging turns and intersections.

Validation: Pearson correlation 0.805 · MAE 0.0277 · RMSE 0.0344 · 5 Hz inference.

NONLINEAR CONTROL & VALIDATION

Model predictive control for vehicle trajectory tracking

Developed kinematic MPC simulations, compared open-loop and closed-loop tracking against recorded GPS routes, and investigated sources of lateral deviation, including state initialization, reference timing, and steering conventions.

An engineering workflow for assessing tracking error, curvature commands, optimization horizon, and closed-loop controller behavior.

Methods: Kinematic vehicle models · nonlinear optimization · trajectory analysis · Python.

ROBOT NAVIGATION · 2022–2023

Autonomous navigation and state estimation

Designed mobile-robot navigation pipelines combining GPS, compass, LiDAR, Extended Kalman filtering, graph search, sampling-based planners, and linearized MPC.

Compared planning approaches and control tradeoffs in simulated navigation scenarios.

Methods: A* · Hybrid A* · RRT* · EKF · LiDAR · MPC.

FINITE-TIME NONLINEAR CONTROL · 2018–2020

Spacecraft attitude control and HIL validation

Designed super-twisting attitude control and a nonlinear disturbance observer for flexible spacecraft subject to uncertainty, disturbances, and actuator saturation. Implemented and evaluated the approach in a hardware-in-the-loop simulator.

Compared alternative output definitions, finite-time stability, and controller performance under uncertainty.

Result: About 30% lower settling time compared with the stated baseline in the original study.

DEEP LEARNING · 2022

NormalNet: normal estimation for 3D point clouds

Extended PointNet using sum-based aggregation and combined local/global features, then evaluated point-wise normal estimation using the PCPNet dataset.

Measured improvements in test loss from 0.063 with global-only features to 0.043 with combined features.

Tools: PyTorch · PointNet · 3D point clouds.

MODEL-BASED CONTROL · 2022

MIMO pole-placement control for battery charging

Derived a nonlinear equivalent-circuit battery model, linearized it into state-space form, and designed a MIMO pole-placement controller to regulate terminal voltage.

Verified controllability and observability and assessed robustness to parameter variation in MATLAB/Simulink.

Tools: MATLAB · Simulink · state feedback · battery modeling.
