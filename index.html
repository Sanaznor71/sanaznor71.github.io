PHD RESEARCHER · UNIVERSITY OF OKLAHOMA

Hi, I'm Yasaman.

I work at the intersection of autonomous driving, robotics, machine learning, and control.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering at the University of Oklahoma, where my research focuses on learning-based motion planning, model predictive control, and making autonomous driving systems more reliable in real-world conditions.

My work connects data-driven modeling with closed-loop validation and vehicle deployment—from training curvature-prediction models to analyzing how controllers behave at intersections. I enjoy research that bridges algorithms, experiments, and practical systems.
