LEARNING-BASED DRIVING · 2026

Curvature prediction for autonomous driving

Developed a GPS-supervised prediction pipeline using Comma 3X driving data and integrated the model into an on-vehicle inference workflow.

Research focus: Held-out curvature correlation r = 0.805; real-time inference at 5 Hz.

Explore project →
PLANNING & CONTROL

Model predictive trajectory tracking

Designed open-loop and closed-loop vehicle simulations around recorded routes to study tracking performance, reference alignment, latency, and steering conventions.

Research focus: Controller diagnosis and systematic trajectory evaluation.

Explore project →
NONLINEAR CONTROL

Finite-time spacecraft attitude control

Designed super-twisting controllers with nonlinear disturbance observers and validated controller behavior in hardware-in-the-loop experiments.

Research focus: Experimental control design under disturbances and actuator constraints.
